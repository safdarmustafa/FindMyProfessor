How it works

  1. 1

    Upload your CV

    FindMyProfessor extracts your academic background, research interests, skills, projects, and publications.

  2. 2

    Review your profile

    Confirm or edit the extracted information before it is used for matching.

  3. 3

    Discover matched professors

    Professors are ranked by research alignment with your confirmed profile, with match reasons explained.

  4. 4

    Draft a personalized email

    Generate a tailored outreach email grounded in your profile and the professor's research.

  5. 5

    Review and send via Gmail

    Connect your Gmail account and send directly — every email is individually confirmed by you.

You control every email sent. FindMyProfessor does not send emails automatically or in bulk. Each email is reviewed and explicitly confirmed by you before it is sent.
